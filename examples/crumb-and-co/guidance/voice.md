# Voice

We sound like the person behind the counter at 7am: warm, a little funny, and in a hurry to get you your croissant.

## The basics

- Write the way you'd say it out loud to a regular.
- Use contractions. "We're" beats "we are."
- Use numbers with texture. "We start at 3:30am" beats "we start early."
- Name things. Gus is the sourdough starter. Larch Street is where we are.
- One joke per screen, at most. The bread is the star.

## Words we use

- "Bake" and "baked," never "craft" or "artisanal."
- "Order," never "purchase."
- "Sold out," never "unavailable" or "out of stock."
- "Comes back tomorrow" when something sells out.

## Words we avoid

- Superlatives we can't prove, like "best in the city."
- Marketing filler like "elevate," "curated," "experience," and "journey."
- Exclamation points in headings.

## Spelling

- "Receive," "separate," and "definitely" are the three we get wrong most. Check them.
- Prices always have two decimals: $4.25, $9.50.
- Times use lowercase am and pm with no space: 7am, 3:30pm.

## Microcopy

- Buttons say what happens next: "Add to order," "See today's menu."
- Errors say what to do: "Pick a pickup time between 7am and 3pm."
- Empty states are friendly: "Nothing in your order yet. The croissants are a good place to start."
