# Arrows

Arrows show movement or point at something. Both kinds follow the same rules.

## Flow arrows

- Flow arrows join objects that happen in order, left to right.
- Use one gentle curve, never straight lines with sharp elbows.
- Stop the arrowhead a little short of its target, about 10 to 16px from the object's visible edge. Never inside it.
- The last bit of the path runs straight into the head, so the head points where the path was going.

## Callout arrows

- A callout arrow starts at the middle of its caption: sideways from the first or last letter, or straight out from the middle of the caption.
- Never start a callout arrow from a corner of the caption or a random spot near it.
- Draw it as one wide, smooth swoop. No straight runs, no hooks right before the head.
- No two callout arrows in a figure should be mirror images. They should look like separate flicks of a pen.

## Color and weight

- All arrows are `--primary` at `--figure-stroke` width.
- Arrowheads are filled, not open.

## Checks

- Every arrow clears every label and icon it passes.
- Every arrowhead stops 10 to 16px short of its target.
- Callout arrows start at the caption's middle.
